## 1A. Depth-Axis Offset — Bragg Peak ~12mm Deeper With Radial Dependence (diagnosis: 2026-06-03)

Root cause confirmed through code audit and `rtd_range_curve_audit.log` trace. Two independent effects combine to produce both the uniform depth translation and the radial dependence observed by the user:

**Effect 1 — uniform 71-step (7.1 mm) offset:** The wrapper's `primTransfDiv` transfer computes BEV depth from axial gantry-Z. The `invertAndShift(_, _, -beamFirstInside)` at `raytracedicom_wrapper.cu:7437` shifts the transfer Z-index by −71. This was correct in upstream RTD-main **only because RTD-main also trims** the BEV 3D texture Z-extent before transfer (`kernel_wrapper.cu:1127-1130`: extent = `beamFirstCalculatedPassive - beamFirstInside`, copy offset = `beamFirstInside * width * height`). The wrapper creates the texture from the full untrimmed buffer (`bevDoseZ = beamFirstGuaranteedPassive = 5071`), so the shift maps doseY to BEV step 71 earlier than carbonPBS → WEPL underestimated by 7.1 mm at every doseY → Bragg peak displaced distally by ~7 mm.

**Effect 2 — off-axis divergent-depth bias (~2 mm extra at fanX≈150 mm):** `TransferParamStructDiv3::getFanIdx` (`include/algorithms/transfer_param_struct_div3.cuh:39-44`) applies divergence correction to X and Y via `normDist` but computes Z from axial gantry-Z only. CarbonPBS evaluates WEPL from divergent projected length: `projectedLength = axialDist · N/sad` where `N = √(fanX²+fanY²+sad²)`. At the Bragg peak depth, this adds ~5 mm extra projected path for edge rays (fanX=150 mm, N/sad≈1.0022), making carbonPBS sample the WEQ/IDD at a deeper index than the wrapper's axial transfer provides. Combined with Effect 1, the total shift at center is ~7 mm and at periphery ~10–12 mm, matching the observed "dose field translated distally, more at edges" signature.

### Evidence chain

| Observation | Root mechanism |
|---|---|
| Whole dose field translated distally | 71-step Z-shift without corresponding BEV texture trim |
| Center ~8mm, periphery ~10-12mm deeper | Axial-only Z + missing divergent-path-length correction |
| `coefOffset.z + globalOffset.z = 9` at doseY=0 | Should be 80 to match carbonPBS `WEQ_idx = 20·doseY + 80` |
| `doseAxisResponse dY=(..., 20)` slope correct | Per-step scaling is ok; only the Z-intercept is wrong |
| Periphery has ~2 mm extra shift beyond center | `N/sad = 1 + r²/(2·sad²)` per-ray correction missing from transfer Z |

### Upstream audit (RayTraceDicom-main)

- **71-step offset: NOT present upstream.** RTD-main trims the BEV texture Z-extent to `beamFirstCalculatedPassive - beamFirstInside` (line 1127) and offsets the copy source by `beamFirstInside * width * height` (line 1130). The `invertAndShift(_, _, -beamFirstInside)` at line 1232 is self-consistent with this trim. The wrapper inherited the `invertAndShift` call but did not port the corresponding texture trim — a porting gap.
- **Divergent Z in `getFanIdx`: same pattern upstream, but impact mitigated.** RTD-main computes WEPL from a CT-based divergent-ray tracer that inherently accounts for off-axis path length per step (each ray's WEPL integral follows its own divergent path). The axial-only Z in the transfer creates a smaller residual error because the per-ray WEPL values are already correct. In the wrapper, the WEQ volume was generated externally by carbonPBS with its own projected-length indexing, so the axial-Z-vs-divergent-WEPL mismatch is more pronounced.

## 6E. Low-Density / Air Voxel Dose, And PB-vs-MC Depth-Zero (investigation 2026-06-09)

User observation on a head-and-neck plan: voxels at `HU=-1000` (air) deposit **zero** dose in subSecond and an **entry-equal constant** in carbonPBS, whereas MC shows a low-but-nonzero, slowly-declining dose through air. Separately, **both** subSecond and carbonPBS place the dose field **deeper than MC**, and the shallowest low-density layers are counted into the dose grid while MC does not build voxels there. These are two distinct problems (6E-A numerical, 6E-B depth-zero) and both are downstream-relevant to 6D.

### 6E-A. Air / low-density voxel dose is wrong in both engines

Confirmed by code audit:
- **subSecond zeroes air dose.** `src/algorithms/idd_sigma.cu:194,197` — `mass = density * stepVol`; the deposit `res = rayWeight * (cumulDose - cumulDoseOld) / mass` is gated by `if (mass > 1e-2f)`. For air, `density≈0.0012` (correct value from `density_Schneider2000_adj.txt[0]`, NOT zero) → `mass≈1e-5 << 1e-2` → guard fails → `res` stays 0. The guard exists to prevent `1/mass` blow-up but its side effect is hard-zeroing air dose. This is the `dose-to-medium` form `dose = ΔE/Δm`; the `#ifdef DOSE_TO_WATER` branch (`idd_sigma.cu:191`, `raytracedicom_wrapper.cu:3570`) uses `mass=(cumulSp-cumulSpOld)*stepVol` (water-equivalent) instead, but the live build uses the medium branch.
- **Removing the guard is NOT a fix (user-verified).** With the `>1e-2` guard removed, air/shallow layers show violent random fluctuation and over-large dose. Root cause: `cumulDose - cumulDoseOld` is a catastrophic-cancellation of two near-equal large IDD-texture samples (air contributes ~0 to `cumulSp`, so adjacent steps read almost the same `cumulIddTex` value), divided by a tiny `mass` → `0/0`-type noise amplified by `1/mass`.
- **carbonPBS freezes the entry IDD value.** `carbonPBS/deviceCalDose.cu:548,570-577` — IDD is sampled at `weqDepth = tex3D(rayweqData, idx, ...)` (cumulative water-equivalent depth) and deposited as `idd * gaussianWeight * overallWeight * subspotweight * nPar` with **no division by density/mass**. Through air, `weqDepth` is nearly frozen (air contributes ~0 WEPL) → the IDD index stalls → consecutive air voxels receive the same near-entry IDD constant → air dose biased high and non-declining.

Tasks:
- [ ] 6E-A.1 Replace subSecond's `if (mass > 1e-2f)` hard-zero with a numerically stable low-density deposit. Preferred direction (MC-aligned): compute the step energy from `Φ × (S/ρ)_medium` (fluence × mass stopping power, which is ρ-independent and air≈water within a few %) instead of `(cumulDose-cumulDoseOld)/mass`, so air gives a low-but-finite, smooth dose without `0/0`. Avoid the two-sample IDD subtraction as the energy source; use the IDD derivative (dose-per-WEPL) × ΔcumulSp, or a density floor `max(mass, ρ_floor·V)` only as a fallback. Decide `DOSE_TO_WATER` vs `dose-to-medium` reporting convention up front (MC refs are usually dose-to-water).
- [ ] 6E-A.2 Decide carbonPBS air handling: either accept the frozen-entry-constant as a known carbonPBS limitation (document it) or, if carbonPBS must align to MC, make the air-region IDD decline with geometric depth instead of freezing on `weqDepth`. Note this converts carbonPBS toward dose-to-medium and is a larger change.
- [ ] 6E-A.3 Quantify against MC: extract a depth-dose profile through an air cavity for one plan and compare subSecond / carbonPBS / MC. Acceptance = air dose low, nonzero, monotonically slowly declining, no random spikes.
